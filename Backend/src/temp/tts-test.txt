What Happens When We Eat?
Narration

We eat yummy food like apples and sandwiches every day. But where does all that food go after we swallow it? Our body has a special system to break down food. This amazing process is called digestion.

Key Concept

Digestion is the process where our body breaks down food into tiny pieces to get energy and nutrients.
